Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: We use the information you submit through our enquiry form to understand your request and respond to you. We do not sell enquiry information.

1. Information we collect

When you submit the website enquiry form, we may collect your name, work email address, business or company name, website address, industry, requested service, approximate monthly advertising budget, message, and basic submission metadata such as the time the enquiry was created.

2. How we use information

  • To read and respond to your enquiry.
  • To understand your business needs and prepare relevant service information or a proposal you request.
  • To maintain and protect the enquiry system and prevent misuse.
  • To keep records of conversations and requests where reasonably necessary.

Submitting the form does not automatically subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

3. Storage and service providers

Enquiry records are stored in AdNova AI's Supabase-hosted database. Supabase provides database and infrastructure services to the website. Access to submitted enquiries is restricted to authorized AdNova AI administrators through access controls. We do not place secret server credentials in the public website code.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information submitted through the enquiry form. We may process information through infrastructure providers that help operate the website, or disclose it where required by applicable law or to protect the service and its users.

5. Retention and deletion requests

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal or security requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion of an enquiry, email adnovaai.business@gmail.com from the address associated with the request. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

6. Security

We use access controls and database policies intended to restrict access to enquiry records. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's information

The enquiry form is intended for business enquiries and is not designed to collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website and services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact adnovaai.business@gmail.com.
